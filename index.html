About us

Made by hand. Made for you.

Ade The Brand makes luxury handmade footwear for women, men and children. Nothing here comes off a factory line. Each pair is cut, stitched and finished by hand, one order at a time.

Because every pair is made to order, you get your own size and the finish you asked for. From everyday slides to statement platforms, the aim is the same: footwear that is comfortable, well made and worth being seen in.

That is what "for the magnificent you" means.
